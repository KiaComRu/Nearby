How to send a file from your phone for free

Open Nearby on both devices, create an invitation and connect using a QR code, link or nine-digit ID. Confirm the device and choose a file on your phone. Receiving starts automatically. Save the received file before closing the page.

Free file transfer in four steps

  1. Open this page on your phone and the receiving device.
  2. Create an invitation and open its link or scan the QR code on the second device. You can also enter all 9 digits of the ID.
  3. Confirm the connection on the inviting device. Choose the recipient and file on your phone.
  4. Wait for the file to arrive, then open or save it from the list. On iPhone and iPad, tap the open-file icon.

Free file transfer questions

How can I send a file from phone to phone for free?

Open Nearby in both phones’ browsers, create an invitation and connect the second phone using a QR code, link or ID. Confirm the device and choose your file. Android and iPhone are supported, including different Wi-Fi networks or mobile data. No app installation or sign-up is required.

How can I send a file from my phone to a PC for free?

Open this page on your phone and Windows PC. Create an invitation on the PC, then scan its QR code with your phone camera or enter the ID. Confirm the connection on the PC and send the file from your phone. No cable or software installation is required.

How can I transfer a file from my phone to a computer for free?

Nearby runs in a browser on Windows, macOS and Linux. Open Nearby on your computer and phone, connect them with an invitation and send your file. File transfer is free and requires neither a USB cable nor an account.

How can I send a file from my phone to a tablet for free?

Open Nearby on your phone and Android tablet or iPad, create an invitation and connect the second device. Choose the file on your phone. On iPad, open the received file from the list and save it using Safari’s sharing menu. Keep both pages open during transfer.

Is Nearby really free?

Yes. The Nearby web app is free: no registration, subscription or transfer fee. Both devices need internet access to connect. Nearby selects a direct connection when available and otherwise uses an encrypted internet channel.

How large a file can I transfer for free?

Normal receiving in a phone browser and the internet channel support up to 100 MiB per file. A direct connection to Chrome or Edge on a PC with a receiving folder selected in advance supports up to 10 GiB. The recipient’s actual limit is shown before sending.

Can I transfer files between different networks for free?

Yes. Nearby automatically checks for a direct connection and falls back to an end-to-end encrypted internet channel when needed. Keep Nearby open on both devices, keep your phone unlocked and save the received file before closing the page.
